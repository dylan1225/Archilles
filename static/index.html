03 Method

Look. Doubt. Calculate.

The model describes. The checks doubt it. The numbers come from engineering charts, not from the model.

  1. 01

    Look

    It lists what is visible on the surface before naming anything: beach marks, splinters, layer lines, a stress raiser.

  2. 02

    Doubt

    Three independent runs must agree. The runner-up mode must be ruled out by a feature in the photo. A photo of nothing broken gets no diagnosis.

  3. 03

    Calculate

    Geometry fixes get a stress concentration factor from Peterson's charts in mechanics.py, so before and after are numbers, not adjectives.

Scales from a 3D-printed bracket to a crack in a building wall.
